How I got started

I got intodrouced to photography when I was 14. A close friend gave me an old Canon Rebel that had been collecting dust. Once I started using it, I couldn’t put it down. While pursuing my military career I did not have the luxury of contuining my passion. Years passed and I was reintrouduced to capturing memories as well as video editing. Since then, I’ve added videography and cinematography to my repetoire. I love to shoot just about anything.

My experience includes employment with CADY Studios and being a film coordinator for Coca-Cola's Classic Christmas. Different settings, different people, and different moments to capture. These opportunities helped me master making memories that will last forever.

When not working behind the lens, I fix cars and motorcycles. Whether I’m in the shop or figuring out a shot, my work ethic is thorough and I make your visions come to life.

I’m straightforward and honest. I’d rather be myself than put on a facade. Not every creative idea fits neatly into a box. If you’ve got something in mind, please share! I'm always up for a challenge and trying something new.
