The creator

Kai Collazo

Photography & Video

mkconfilm.

A friend gave me a dusty old Canon Rebel. I couldn’t put it down.

That camera got me hooked. Since then, I’ve been exploring photography, videography, and cinematography. I enjoy capturing just about anything—there isn’t one subject or style I want to limit myself to.

My experience includes work with CADY Studios and photographing Santa at Coca-Cola Classic Christmas. Different settings, different people, and different moments to capture.

Outside of the camera, I work on cars and motorcycles. I’m also a Navy veteran. Whether I’m working on a machine or figuring out a shot, I like being hands-on and making things happen.

I’m pretty straightforward. I’d rather be myself than put on a polished act, and I don’t think every creative idea needs to fit neatly into a box. If you’ve got something in mind, tell me about it.
